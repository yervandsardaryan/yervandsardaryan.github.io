Business context

Businesses need to act on incoming requests quickly while applying consistent policy decisions. AI/ML outputs can inform decisions, while business-configurable rules and rate cards support eligibility, pricing and policy control. The BA challenge is to turn those goals into testable workflows, data requirements and edge-case behavior.

In this illustrative workflow, a requesting system submits input data; the system validates required attributes, obtains a model score, evaluates eligibility rules and selects a configured rate tier before returning an outcome. Key considerations for an enterprise-ready design

Configuration governance

Role-based access, approval/version history, effective dates and rollback for policies and rate cards.

Operational reliability

Latency expectations, request correlation, bounded retries and documented failures.

Decision traceability

Audit-friendly records of model version, policy version, decision reason and decision timestamp, subject to retention requirements.
