Business challenge & solution

Financial data arrives from different external parties in different structures. Downstream consumers benefit from consistent, validated records rather than having to interpret each provider-specific format independently.

The product receives external data feeds, standardizes and aggregates them, and makes the processed information available through outbound APIs. This explanation is intentionally high level.
